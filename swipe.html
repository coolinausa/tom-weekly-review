How to Swipe

Find videos that are blowing up. Save them. Make your own version.

1

Where to look

  • Instagram Reels and TikTok. Search brisket, steak, beef ribs, BBQ.
  • Follow 30 to 50 BBQ and steak creators. Your feed will show you their best videos every day.
  • Look outside BBQ too. An idea that goes viral in cooking, or anywhere, can work with meat.
2

What to save

Save videos that did way better than that creator's normal.

Big creator

Usual video 1M
This video 1M
Normal day. Skip.

Small creator

Usual video 10K
This video 500K
50× their normal. Save it.
3

How to save

  • Save it on your Instagram, in a folder called Swipe.
  • Know why you saved it: the first second, the text, or the audio?
4

Make your version

  • Keep the idea. Change the meat, the place, and the style to yours.
  • Copy how it's built: the first second, the text on screen, the audio, the length.

Make your own video. Never repost someone else's.

5

How many

  • Save about 5 a day, in the morning at the office.
  • Each week, pick the best 2 or 3 and film your versions.
