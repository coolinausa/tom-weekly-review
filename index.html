Tom's Weekly Review

30 minutes. Find your winners. Then make more of them.

The goal

Views first

Your best videos become hooks for Coolina ads.

Then the rest

Followers, shares and comments come next.

Winners only

Skip the videos that did badly.

The 6 steps

  1. 1NumbersInsights, last 7 days. Write views, new followers, profile visits.
  2. 2WinnersContent, sort by Views. Pick your top 5.
  3. 3QuestionsAnswer the 5 questions below for each winner.
  4. 4Watch timeIf you can see it: on your #1 video, where do people leave?
  5. 5PatternWhat do your winners have in common?
  6. 6PlanPick what to make next week. Ideas below.

The 5 questions

  1. 1What happens in the first second?
  2. 2Is there text on screen? What does it say?
  3. 3What meat or cook is it?
  4. 4What sound is on it? Trending audio, cooking sounds, or your voice?
  5. 5What stands out, compared to your other videos?The big one

Ideas for next week

No-brainers

Film new videos like your winners, with small changes.

Re-edit

Make new videos from your winners' footage.

New text

Try different texts on screen.

Trending audio

Try a trending song. Look for the small arrow next to it.

Mix them.One video can use two or three ideas. Re-edit a winner, add new text, put a trending audio on it. Or take the original video and just add new text and audio.
